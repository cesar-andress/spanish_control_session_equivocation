\section{Data and Method}
\label{sec:data-method}

% Planned workflow (future tense). Nothing below reports completed empirical work.

This study is designed as a human annotation project on Spanish parliamentary
control sessions. The intended coding unit is the triple formed by a
pre-registered oral question, the questioner's opening turn, and the Prime
Minister's first response. The intended central construct is reply status
(explicit reply, intermediate reply, or non-reply), conceptually aligned with
the Bull reply/non-reply tradition; operational definitions remain to be frozen
in a codebook before main annotation.

The planned sequence is development, calibration, protocol freeze, independent
main annotation by two expert annotators, reliability estimation, adjudication
only after reliability is committed, and then analysis of a pre-specified
primary endpoint: the risk difference in explicit-reply rates across a
pre-defined political-alignment contrast of the questioner's parliamentary
group. Independent labels will be preserved separately from adjudicated labels.
No corpus acquisition, sampling, or annotation has been completed at the time
of this scaffold.
