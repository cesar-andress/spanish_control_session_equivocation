\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

% Interpretive discussion after results exist. Pre-outcome notes only below.

Several limitations are known before outcomes exist. The design is
observational and descriptive: formation-vote alignment is an institutional
baseline, not a treatment. Confrontational question formulation is strongly
imbalanced by alignment, so any later alignment difference in explicit-reply
rates cannot be read as a clean side effect. Linkage attrition leaves
unlinked and ambiguous exchanges outside the eligible set; missingness is not
assumed random. The instrument remains under calibration; reliability and
main-study estimates will be reported only when those data exist.

External validity is limited to XIV prime-ministerial control sessions inside
ParlaMint-ES coverage. The study does not evaluate ministerial substitutes,
written questions, or other legislatures as primary targets.
