\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper specifies a pre-outcome measurement design for reply explicitness in
Spanish prime-ministerial control sessions: a linked registered+Q1+R1 unit, a
three-way human reply-status instrument, a formation-vote descriptive grouping,
and a main frame of 97 XIV exchanges after restoring linkage-QA units that were
never reply-coded. Empirical reply-rate results are reserved until calibration
closes and main annotation is complete.
