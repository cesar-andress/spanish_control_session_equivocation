\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper specifies a pre-outcome measurement design for reply explicitness in
Spanish prime-ministerial control sessions: a linked registered+Q1+R1 unit, a
three-way human reply-status instrument, a formation-vote descriptive grouping,
and a main frame of 100 XIV exchanges after restoring linkage-QA and Phase-0
probe units that were never reply-coded. Empirical reply-rate results are
reserved until calibration closes and main annotation is complete.
