# Changes from reply_status codebook v0.2 → v0.3

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** DRAFT instrument revision after Phase 4.3 discussion.  
**Not frozen.** Development interpretations are not gold.

Source evidence: independent development solutions; joint discussion return
(`phase43_disagreement_discussion_return.json`);
`PHASE43_DISAGREEMENT_DISCUSSION_RETURN.md`.

---

## Summary

v0.3 keeps the three-way reply status and the Q1-judged / registered-anchor
unit. It clarifies topical relevance versus communicative responsiveness,
adds a discourse-semantic generic-reference rule, strengthens packet display
validation, refines multi-demand and premise-rejection guidance, documents
political-bias safeguards, and preserves Case 7 as an unresolved boundary.

---

## Change 1 — Mandatory three-text display and packet validation

**Previous (v0.2):** Unit defined as registered + Q1 + R1, but no formal rule
that human packets must always *display* the registered question.

**New (v0.3 §1.1):** Every development, calibration and main unit must show
pregunta registrada → pregunta oral → primera respuesta, in that order.
Missing registered text must be flagged before annotation. Packet validation
fails on empty/whitespace missing fields.

**Evidence:** Cases 12–13 discussion: experts attributed disagreement mainly
to omission of the registered question in the first Word booklet.

**Type:** Clarifies / adds an **instrument-presentation** rule (not a change
to the linguistic category definitions themselves).

---

## Change 2 — Topical relevance vs communicative demand

**Previous (v0.2):** Non-reply included “generic government-record speech that
never contacts the oral proposition,” but the guide did not foreground the
explicit contrast *same topic* vs *answer the ask*.

**New (v0.3 §3):** Explicit distinction: hablar del mismo tema ≠ responder a
la cuestión planteada. Topical continuity alone is insufficient.

**Evidence:** Case 1 discussion (explicit oral ask vs thematic content);
general discussion on concreteness; Cases 6–7 thematic housing/LGTBI talk.

**Type:** Clarifies decision rule (boundary between partial and absence).

---

## Change 3 — Generic-reference rule

**Previous (v0.2):** No dedicated rule for generic nouns such as *ley*,
*medida*, *propuesta*, *fórmula*.

**New (v0.3 §3.3):** Lexical/topical overlap does not establish
responsiveness; generic reference counts only when the surrounding
proposition addresses the requested issue. Explicitly **not** a keyword rule.

**Evidence:** Cases 7 and 13 discussion; experts’ list of generic words;
general discussion on digressive reuse of shared vocabulary.

**Type:** Clarifies decision rule (discourse-semantic criterion).

---

## Change 4 — Refined category definitions (Spanish human labels)

**Previous (v0.2 §5):** English definitions centred on “information slot.”

**New (v0.3 §6):** Same three internal values; human-facing definitions in
academic Spanish; partial category redefined so it is **not** “somewhat
related”; absence explicitly allowed despite topical continuity.

**Evidence:** Experts’ difficulty at the partial/absence boundary; request
for concreteness relative to the communicative demand.

**Type:** Clarifies wording; same three-category architecture.

---

## Change 5 — Multi-demand table

**Previous (v0.2 §2.2):** Answering only one distinct ask → at most
intermediate, unless restatement.

**New (v0.3 §7):** Explicit three-row table (all / some / none of central
components). Warns against counting every rhetorical interrogative.

**Evidence:** General discussion on multi-item questions; Case 1 secondary
themes vs main ask.

**Type:** Clarifies / slightly strengthens operationalisation of existing rule.

---

## Change 6 — Quantitative / qualitative co-equal axes

**Previous (v0.2):** Mixed forms coded by dominant ask; no worked rule for
joint quantitative + evaluative demands.

**New (v0.3 §7.1):** If both axes are co-equal and only one is addressed →
normally partial. Do not invent a mechanical universal when one axis is
clearly subordinate. Dual independent category lists proposed by experts are
**not** adopted.

**Evidence:** Experts proposed separate quantitative/qualitative lists; v0.3
absorbs the substance as a worked multi-demand rule without multiplying
outcome variables.

**Type:** Clarifies; deliberately **does not** change the outcome schema.

---

## Change 7 — Premise-rejection (cautious)

**Previous (v0.2 hard-case table):** Contests presupposition and answers →
explicit; contests only → usually intermediate or non-reply.

**New (v0.3 §8):** Same spirit, marked as **known calibration boundary**;
development discussion left the issue partly open (absence vs ideological
positioning).

**Evidence:** Experts’ premise-rejection note; prior EXPECTED disagreement
report.

**Type:** Clarifies; does **not** claim the boundary is solved.

---

## Change 8 — Known frontier case (Case 7)

**Previous (v0.2):** No named unresolved development case.

**New (v0.3 §10):** Case 7 documented as known frontier (rent bubble vs
generic housing law); both expert readings remain plausible; **no** forced
category; stays outside calibration and main evaluation.

**Evidence:** Joint discussion left optional agreed interpretation unmarked.

**Type:** Documentation / scope control (not a new decision rule forcing
agreement).

---

## Change 9 — Political-bias safeguards

**Previous (v0.2):** Implicit via blinded packets; no dedicated section.

**New (v0.3 §9):** Explicit safeguards; acknowledges incomplete blinding when
political content is inside source text.

**Evidence:** Jose Jaime’s development feedback; Phase 4.3 general discussion
on political context.

**Type:** Methodological clarification.

---

## Change 10 — Orientative examples (not gold)

**Previous (v0.2):** Hard-case table without development-linked orientative
examples.

**New (v0.3 §11):** Orientative examples derived from development material,
labelled as non-gold; Case 13-like wording marked as boundary.

**Evidence:** Development cases 1, 6, 12, 13 and discussion.

**Type:** Clarifies training material; does not convert development
interpretations into gold.

---

## Explicit non-changes

- Still three `reply_status` values only.  
- Still Q1-judged with registered anchor.  
- Still no allies/adversaries analytic labels.  
- Still no calibration seeds / sample draw.  
- Still no XIV main outcomes.  
- Original independent development solution files unchanged.  
- `reply_status_codebook_v0.2.md` unchanged.
